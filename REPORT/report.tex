\date{23 September 2026}

\begin{abstract}\noindent
In the KLOE large-angle (KLOE-LA) scenario the NLO predictions of \ceex{} and \phok{}~10.0
for $e^+e^-\to\pi^+\pi^-\gamma$ agree in the total cross section and in every charge-even
distribution, but disagree in the pion polar angles: the forward--backward asymmetry of the
$\pi^+$ is $\Ath=0.2375$ in \ceex{} (and $0.2372$ in \bby) against $0.2787$ in \phok.
We show that the difference is entirely due to two errors in the one-photon ISR$\times$FSR
interference at NLO in \phok{} (routine \code{ampLO}): a
misplaced parenthesis that adds three helicity terms of the tree interference a second time,
and a block that keeps the tree interference together with an analytic ISR soft function that
the calculation replaces by the eikonal elsewhere. The second error makes the \phok{}
asymmetry depend on the unphysical soft-photon cutoff. A point-by-point comparison of the
one-photon matrix elements shows that the two errors account for the whole charge-odd
difference to $2.5\times10^{-5}$ of the tree. With both corrected, \phok{} gives
$\Ath=\HiAav$ averaged over the cutoff and $\FiveA$ with $5\times10^9$ events, independent of
the cutoff; \ceex{} (\CeexA) and \bby{} ($0.23724$) agree with it to $3\times10^{-5}$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Summary of findings}
\label{sec:summary}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.,leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{The disagreement is reproduced} with a fresh \phok{} build and fresh \ceex{} runs
  (\cref{sec:discrepancy}): totals agree to $10^{-4}$, charge-even shapes agree, and only
  $\theta^\pm$ and $\theta_{\rm avg}$ differ ($\chi^2/{\rm ndf}\approx 10^3$). At LO the two
  codes agree exactly.
\item \textbf{The \phok{} asymmetry depends on the soft cutoff $w$}: $\Ath$ changes by
  $+0.0156$ per decade of $w$ while $\sigma$ is constant (\cref{sec:t1}).
\item \textbf{The whole effect sits in the charge-odd part of the one-photon contribution}
  (Born + virtual + soft); the hard two-photon emission agrees between the codes
  (\cref{sec:t3}).
\item \textbf{Two errors in \phok{} explain it point by point} (\cref{sec:t4,sec:bugs}):
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{H1} -- \code{phokhara\_10.0.f:9969--9982}: the factor $(v_f+v_s)$ multiplies only
    the last of four helicity terms of the tree interference;
  \item \textbf{H2} -- block B5, the ISR one-loop current times the FSR tree
    (\code{phokhara\_10.0.f:9998--10007}; \code{ISRcurrent\_no\_mass\_terms.f:134,210}):
    an extra ${\rm Re}[F^*I]\,(1+S_{\rm ISR}(w))$.
  \end{itemize}
  Subtracting both leaves a per-point residual of $2.5\times10^{-5}$ of the tree.
\item \textbf{With both corrected}, $\Ath$ no longer depends on $w$ and agrees with \ceex{} and
  \bby{}, in total and separately in the one- and two-photon parts (\cref{sec:fix}). Totals and
  charge-even distributions are unchanged.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Scenario.} KLOE-LA (Strong2020 KLOE-I), $\sqrt s=1.02$~GeV:
$50^\circ\le\theta_\pm\le130^\circ$; $|p^z_\pm|>90$~MeV or $p^\perp_\pm>160$~MeV; at least one
photon with $50^\circ\le\theta_\gamma\le130^\circ$ and $E_\gamma>20$~MeV;
$0.1\le M_{\pi\pi}^2\le0.85$~GeV$^2$. Angles are measured from the electron beam in both codes.
Histograms: the 18 KLOE-I observables with 600 uniform bins. The forward--backward asymmetry is
$A=[\sigma(\theta<90^\circ)-\sigma(\theta>90^\circ)]/\sigma$.

\paragraph{Codes.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{\ceex-main} (commit \code{e05d007}): \code{pipig --scenario=KLOE-LA --NLO}, one-loop
  amplitudes from \gosam{} (\code{eepipig\_noVP\_1L\_onshell}, \code{eepipigg}), YFS soft
  subtraction with cutoff $E_{\min}=10^{-5}$~GeV ($10^{-4}$~GeV only in the cutoff checks); 5 MPI ranks per task.
\item \textbf{\phok~10.0} (\code{PHOKARA\_10.0} @ \code{133d68b}) with the KLOE-I cut file,
  card \code{nlo=nlo2=1}, IFSNLO$=1$, ISR+INT+FSR, \code{ivac=0}, \code{FF\_pion=-1} ($F_\pi=1$),
  no GVMD, no $f_0$, soft cutoff $w=10^{-5}$ (soft region $E_\gamma<w\sqrt s$). The production
  binary \code{phokhara\_nostop} only disables the abort on ``Max(i) too small''
  (\cref{sec:other}); only weighted events are used.
\item \textbf{\bby} and the earlier \phok{} reference set (5~Aug) are taken from the Strong2020
  comparison files.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Pipeline.} SLURM arrays per run name; \ceex{} rank outputs are screened with
\code{check\_total.sh} and outliers are moved aside (\code{quarantine.sh}) before merging;
\code{plot\_compare.py} produces the distribution and ratio plots. Everything is in the
repository (\cref{app:files}).

\section{\texorpdfstring{How \phok{} computes $\pi^+\pi^-\gamma$ at NLO}{How PHOKHARA computes pi+pi-gamma at NLO}}
\label{sec:phok}

This section describes what \phok~10.0 computes for $\pi^+\pi^-\gamma$ at full NLO, the
calculation of this report ($F_\pi=1$, no vacuum polarisation): first the overall structure, then the routines, the UV and IR treatment, and
finally the two blocks where the errors are (\cref{sec:phok-errors}). Line numbers refer to the
unpatched \code{phokhara\_10.0.f} unless another file is named. Statements marked
\emph{(inferred)} are our reading of the code; the code itself does not document them.

\subsection{Overall structure}
\label{sec:phok-overview}

\paragraph{Amplitudes.} At tree level the one-photon amplitude is $M=I+F$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $I$ (ISR): the photon is emitted from the $e^\pm$ line, and $\gamma^*(q^2)\to\pi\pi$ with
  the current $J^\mu=(q_+-q_-)^\mu F_\pi(q^2)$;
\item $F$ (FSR): $\gamma^*(s)\to\pi\pi\gamma$ in scalar QED, i.e.\ emission from $\pi^+$, from
  $\pi^-$ and the seagull (point-like pions).
\end{itemize}
$|I|^2$ and $|F|^2$ are charge-even; the interference $2{\rm Re}[I^*F]$ is charge-odd (odd under
$\pi^+\leftrightarrow\pi^-$) and is the only source of the pion forward--backward asymmetry. At
NLO every one-loop and soft term is either charge-even or charge-odd; \cref{tab:phok-map} lists
them all.

\paragraph{Event classes.} Two parameters split the photon phase space:
\begin{itemize}
\item $w$, the soft-photon cutoff: a photon with $E_\gamma<w\sqrt s$ (CM frame) is soft, is never
  generated, and is integrated analytically;
\item \code{gmin}, the minimum energy of the tagged photon.
\end{itemize}
Each event is a one-photon or a two-photon event; one random number picks the class with
probability proportional to its maximum weight from the scan pass.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{One-photon events} (one hard photon above \code{gmin}) carry in a single weight the
  tree $|M|^2$, all one-loop virtual corrections, and the integrated soft emission.
\item \textbf{Two-photon events} carry the exact tree $|M|^2$ for $e^+e^-\to\pi^+\pi^-\gamma\gamma$:
  one photon is tagged ($E\ge$\,\code{gmin}, inside the angular cuts), the other only has to be
  above $w\sqrt s$.
\end{itemize}
The two classes add up to the full $\mathcal O(\alpha)$ correction, and the $w$ dependence must
cancel in the sum. This is phase-space slicing; there are no local subtraction terms.

\paragraph{Precision.} The one-photon kinematics are generated in quad precision
(\code{momenta\_quad.f}). Only \code{virt\_pions} uses the quad momenta; all other pieces use
rounded double copies.

\subsection{The one-photon weight}
\label{sec:phok-1ph}

\paragraph{Notation.} Write the one-photon amplitude to $\mathcal O(e^5)$ as
\[
  M = \underbrace{I+F}_{\rm tree}
    + \underbrace{\delta I_e+\delta I_\pi+\delta F_e+\delta F_\pi+\delta B_e+\delta B_\pi}_{\rm one\ loop},
\]
where the one-loop amplitudes are
\begin{itemize}
\item $\delta I_e$: ISR with the loop on the electron line (vertex and self-energy corrections
  around the emitted photon);
\item $\delta I_\pi$: ISR with the $\gamma^*\pi\pi$ vertex correction at $q^2$;
\item $\delta F_e$: FSR with the $e^+e^-\gamma^*$ vertex correction at $s$;
\item $\delta F_\pi$: FSR with the loop on the pion line (self-energies, vertices, seagull);
\item $\delta B_e$, $\delta B_\pi$: two-photon exchange between the electron and pion lines
  (boxes and pentagons), with the real photon on the electron or on the pion line.
\end{itemize}
\Cref{fig:phok-diagrams} shows one example diagram for each class.

The two vertex corrections factorise: $\delta F_e=\tfrac12v_s\,F$ and $\delta I_\pi=\tfrac12v_f\,I$,
with $v_s$ (\code{ver\_s}) and $v_f$ (\code{ver\_f}$=$\code{vertLO}) real $|M|^2$-level factors.

\paragraph{From the amplitude expansion to the routines.} Squaring $M$ and keeping
$\mathcal O(\alpha)$ relative to the tree, every term is an interference $2{\rm Re}[X^*Y]$ of a
tree amplitude $X\in\{I,F\}$ with a tree or one-loop amplitude $Y$. \phok{} computes them in
four routines:
\begin{align}
|M|^2 ={}& \underbrace{|I|^2+2{\rm Re}[I^*\delta I_e]}_{\code{amplit}}\notag\\[2pt]
&+\underbrace{\underbrace{|F|^2+2{\rm Re}[F^*\delta F_e]}_{\rm B1}
  +\underbrace{2{\rm Re}[I^*\delta I_\pi]}_{\rm B2}
  +\underbrace{2{\rm Re}[I^*F]}_{\rm B3}
  +\underbrace{2{\rm Re}[I^*\delta F_e+F^*\delta I_\pi]}_{\rm B4}
  +\underbrace{2{\rm Re}[F^*\delta I_e]}_{\rm B5}}_{\code{helicityampLO}}\notag\\[2pt]
&+\underbrace{2{\rm Re}\big[(I+F)^*(\delta F_\pi+\delta B_e+\delta B_\pi)\big]}_{\code{virt\_pions}}
 +\underbrace{|I+F|^2\,\mathcal S(w)}_{\code{NLOpions}},
\label{eq:phok-split}
\end{align}
where $\mathcal S(w)$ is the eikonal factor for soft emission below $w\sqrt s$. The same
assignment as a grid, one cell per $2{\rm Re}[X^*Y]$ (the $F^*I$ cell is the same term as $I^*F$):
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}l|cc|cccc|c@{}}
\toprule
 & $I$ & $F$ & $\delta I_e$ & $\delta I_\pi$ & $\delta F_e$ & $\delta F_\pi$ & $\delta B_e$, $\delta B_\pi$ \\
\midrule
$I^*$ & \code{amplit} & B3 & \code{amplit} & B2 & B4 & \code{virt\_pions} & \code{virt\_pions} \\
$F^*$ & (B3) & B1 & B5 & B4 & B1 & \code{virt\_pions} & \code{virt\_pions} \\

The pattern follows from what each routine can do:
\begin{itemize}
\item \code{amplit} is a tensor contraction $L^{\mu\nu}H_{\mu\nu}$, which only exists for
  $|{\rm ISR}|^2$: it gives the $I^*$ entries with $I$ and $\delta I_e$, and nothing else.
\item The vertex corrections factorise, $\delta F_e=\tfrac12v_sF$ and $\delta I_\pi=\tfrac12v_fI$,
  so every cell with them is a tree helicity sum times $v_s$ or $v_f$. Together with the tree
  $|F|^2$ and $2{\rm Re}[I^*F]$ they are all in \code{helicityampLO} (B1--B4).
\item $\delta I_e$ appears twice. Against $I^*$ it is in \code{amplit} (analytic, $m_e$ kept);
  against $F^*$ it needs the amplitude, which \code{helicityampLO} takes from
  \code{NLO\_ISR\_no\_mass\_terms} (massless electrons) in B5.
\item The non-factorising loops $\delta F_\pi$, $\delta B_e$, $\delta B_\pi$ are genuine one-loop
  amplitudes, computed in \code{virt\_pions} against the full tree $I+F$.
\end{itemize}
\Cref{tab:phok-map} lists the same terms with their charge factors. Counting charges
($I\propto Q_e^2Q_\pi$, $F\propto Q_eQ_\pi^2$, each loop adds two charges), a term is charge-odd
when it has an odd power of $Q_\pi$. The charge-odd cells are $I^*F$, $I^*\delta F_e$,
$F^*\delta I_\pi$, $F^*\delta I_e$, $I^*\delta F_\pi$, $I^*\delta B_e$ and $F^*\delta B_\pi$, plus
the odd part of the soft term.

\paragraph{\code{amplit}: first line of \cref{eq:phok-split}} (lines 2069, 6757--7037). Starting
from the definition:
\begin{align*}
\code{amplit}
&= L^{\mu\nu}H_{\mu\nu}
  && \text{definition: leptonic $\times$ hadronic tensor}\\
&= |I|^2+\big(2{\rm Re}[I^*\delta I_e]\big)_{\rm V+S}
  && \text{\code{Bornvirtualsoft}: LO + virtual + analytic soft}\\
&= |I|^2+2{\rm Re}[I^*\delta I_e]_{\rm V}+S_{\rm ISR}(w)\,|I|^2
  && \text{split V+S into virtual and soft}\\
&\longrightarrow |I|^2+2{\rm Re}[I^*\delta I_e]_{\rm V}
  && \text{subtract $S_{\rm ISR}(w)\,|I|^2$ (lines 6838--6856)}
\end{align*}
The ingredients:
\begin{itemize}
\item $H^{\mu\nu}\propto|F_\pi(q^2)|^2(q_+-q_-)^\mu(q_+-q_-)^\nu$ (\code{HadronicTensorISR}): the
  $\gamma^*\to\pi\pi$ vertex, squared.
\item $L^{\mu\nu}$ (\code{LeptonicTensor1ISR}): the electron line with the emitted photon,
  squared and summed over the photon polarisation, $L=L_{\rm LO}+\frac{\alpha}{\pi}L_{\rm NLO}$.
  The coefficients come from \code{Bornvirtualsoft} (G.~Rodrigo et al., EPJC~22 (2001) 81;
  J.H.~K\"uhn, G.~Rodrigo, EPJC~25 (2002) 215), with $m_e$ kept. $L_{\rm LO}H=|I|^2$, and
  $\frac{\alpha}{\pi}L_{\rm NLO}H$ is the IR-finite sum of the one-loop ISR correction and the
  analytic soft emission below $w\sqrt s$.
\item V: the virtual correction with its IR pole dropped (dimensional regularisation,
  $\mu^2=s$), the convention of the eikonal integrals in \code{NLOpions}; \emph{(inferred)} from
  the form of $S_{\rm ISR}$.
\item $S_{\rm ISR}(w)$: the analytic ISR soft function (\cref{sec:phok-ir}). Once it is
  subtracted, the soft emission it stood for is supplied by \code{NLOpions}.
\end{itemize}
\code{amplit} contains nothing else: no FSR, no interference, and not the pion-vertex correction
(that is B2).

\paragraph{\code{helicityampLO} $\to$ \code{ampLO}: second line of \cref{eq:phok-split}}
(lines 9687--10021). Starting from the definition,
\[
  \code{helicityampLO}=\frac{(4\pi\alpha)^3}{4}\,\code{ampLO}\times\text{(phase space)},\qquad
  \code{ampLO}=\sum_{\lambda_\gamma=1,2}\big(B_1+B_2+B_3+B_4+B_5\big),
\]
where the $1/4$ is the $e^\pm$ spin average and B1--B5 are helicity sums
$\langle X,Y\rangle$ of tree and one-loop amplitudes (defined below, \cref{tab:phok-blocks}).
Per photon polarisation:
\begin{align*}
\sum_iB_i
&= |F|^2(1+v_s)+|I|^2v_f+2{\rm Re}\langle I,F\rangle
   +{\rm Re}\langle I,F\rangle(v_f+v_s)+{\rm Re}\langle F,I_{\rm 1L}\rangle+\Delta B_4\\
\intertext{\small\emph{$\downarrow$ factorised vertex corrections, $\delta F_e=\tfrac12v_sF$ and
  $\delta I_\pi=\tfrac12v_fI$}}
&= |F|^2+2{\rm Re}[F^*\delta F_e]+2{\rm Re}[I^*\delta I_\pi]+2{\rm Re}[I^*F]
   +2{\rm Re}[I^*\delta F_e+F^*\delta I_\pi]+{\rm Re}\langle F,I_{\rm 1L}\rangle+\Delta B_4\\
\intertext{\small\emph{$\downarrow$ one-loop ISR amplitude of B5,
  $I_{\rm 1L}=I(1+S_{\rm ISR})+2\delta I_e$, so
  ${\rm Re}\langle F,I_{\rm 1L}\rangle=2{\rm Re}[F^*\delta I_e]+\Delta B_5$}}
&= \big[\text{second line of \cref{eq:phok-split}}\big]+\Delta B_4+\Delta B_5\\
\intertext{\small\emph{$\downarrow$ the fix (H1, H2) removes $\Delta B_4$ and $\Delta B_5$}}
&\longrightarrow \big[\text{second line of \cref{eq:phok-split}}\big].
\end{align*}
The first line is the code block by block (\cref{tab:phok-blocks}), with B4 written as its
intended form plus the remainder $\Delta B_4$ left by the misplaced parenthesis. The two extra
terms are both proportional to the tree interference:
\[
  \Delta B_4={\rm Re}\sum_{k\le3}T_k(I)^*T_k(F)\,\big(1-(v_f+v_s)\big),\qquad
  \Delta B_5={\rm Re}\langle I,F\rangle\,\big(1+S_{\rm ISR}(w)\big).
\]
The decomposition of $I_{\rm 1L}$ is read from \code{V1(1)} (\cref{sec:h2}) and confirmed
numerically by the per-point test (\cref{sec:t4}).

\subsection{UV renormalisation}
\label{sec:phok-uv}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Analytic pieces.} \code{Bornvirtualsoft}, $v_s$ and $v_f$ (\code{vertLO}) are
  closed-form, on-shell renormalised results: electron vertex at $s$ and point-like pion form
  factor at $q^2$, normalised to the charge $e$ at $q^2=0$. No counterterm appears in the code;
  with vacuum polarisation off, $\alpha$ does not run.
\item \textbf{Pion-line loops} (\code{test\_vFSR.f:740--761}). This is the only place with
  explicit counterterms: on-shell scalar-QED counterterms built from $B_0$, $C_0$ and the
  derivative $B_0'(m_\pi^2)$ (wave-function renormalisation), multiplied by tree helicity
  structures (\code{diag1}, \code{diag2}) and added to the result
  (\code{counter1}, \code{counter3}, \code{counter8}, \code{counter95}). The crossed version has
  no \code{diag1} analogue.
\item \textbf{Boxes and pentagons.} The two-photon-exchange set is UV-finite and has no
  counterterms. Tensor integrals are reduced with Passarino--Veltman in quad precision; scalar
  integrals come from QCDLoop.
\item \textbf{Regularisation.} Dimensional regularisation with $\mu^2=s$; the $1/\epsilon$
  poles are dropped and the finite parts kept. The IR poles cancel against the soft integrals,
  written in the same convention with $\lambda=\sqrt s$. Scale shifts give $\ln(q^2/s)$ terms,
  e.g.\ the extra $-\tfrac12\ln(q^2/s)$ in \code{qjqj\_sub} and \code{q1q2\_sub}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{IR treatment}
\label{sec:phok-ir}

\paragraph{Slicing.} Soft region $E_\gamma<w\sqrt s$ (CM frame): eikonal approximation,
integrated analytically in \code{NLOpions}. Hard region: exact two-photon matrix element
(\code{gen\_2ph}), with the ISR$\cdot$ISR (\code{ma}/\code{mb}), ISR$\cdot$FSR (\code{mapi}) and
FSR$\cdot$FSR (\code{Jplus}/\code{Jmin}) amplitudes added coherently and squared with the same
$T_1$--$T_4$ pattern.

\paragraph{Soft subtraction in the analytic pieces.} The three analytic one-loop results (ISR in
the leptonic tensor, $v_s$ for the electron vertex, \code{vertLO} for the pion vertex) are
``virtual + soft'' formulas, each with its own analytic $\ln(2w)$ soft function. All soft
emission is supplied by the eikonal integrals of \code{NLOpions}, which also contain the $e\pi$
terms, so the analytic soft function is removed from each of the three:
\begin{itemize}
\item leptonic tensor (lines 6838--6856): $L-L_{\rm LO}\,S_{\rm ISR}(w)$, with
  \[
  S_{\rm ISR}(w)=\frac{\alpha}{\pi}\Big[-\ln(4w^2)\,(1+L)-\tfrac12L^2-L-\tfrac{\pi^2}{3}\Big],
  \qquad L=\ln\frac{m_e^2}{s};
  \]
\item $v_s$ (lines 2754--2761): $v_s-S_{\rm ISR}(w)$; the $\ln(2w)$ terms cancel exactly;
\item \code{vertLO} (line 9795): virtual part minus the soft function of Nucl.\ Phys.\ B57 (1973)
  381, eq.~(29), ``without terms proportional to Log(2w)''.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{What must hold.} After these subtractions every one-loop piece should be free of analytic
$w$ terms. All $w$ dependence then comes from \code{NLOpions} and cancels against the
two-photon events. A leftover analytic $\ln w$ term does not cancel, and the result depends on
$w$.

\subsection{Where the errors are}
\label{sec:phok-errors}

Both errors are in \code{ampLO}, in the two blocks of \cref{tab:phok-blocks} that correct the
tree interference, and both add a multiple of the tree interference (the $\Delta B_4$, $\Delta B_5$
of \cref{sec:phok-1ph}):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{B4 (H1).} The vertex factor $(v_f+v_s)$ is placed inside \code{dreal()} after the
  last helicity term, so it multiplies only $T_4$. $T_1$--$T_3$ of the tree interference are
  added a second time with coefficient 1. $T_3$ is a leading helicity amplitude, so this is an
  $\mathcal O(1)$ extra charge-odd term, not an $\mathcal O(\alpha)$ one.
\item \textbf{B5 (H2).} \code{NLO\_ISR\_no\_mass\_terms} returns the ISR amplitude
  \emph{including} the tree, not the loop alone: its leading coefficient \code{V1(1)} contains
  the tree ``1'' and the analytic soft function $S_{\rm ISR}(w)$. The ``1'' repeats half of B3,
  and $S_{\rm ISR}(w)$ is the one analytic soft function that is never removed, which breaks
  the condition of \cref{sec:phok-ir}.
\end{itemize}
Both extra terms are proportional to ${\rm Re}\langle I,F\rangle$, which is charge-odd. They
integrate to zero over the charge-symmetric KLOE-LA cuts, so $\sigma$ and all charge-even
distributions are unaffected; only the pion polar angles move. \Cref{sec:bugs} gives the code and
the fixes.

\paragraph{Oddities without effect here.} \code{soft\_f} in \code{vertLO} and \code{ver\_f} in
\code{ampNLO} are computed but not used. The virtual-FSR term in \code{virt\_pions} has an
explicit prefactor four times that of the pentagons ($32\pi^2\alpha^4$ vs $8\pi^2\alpha^4$); we
did not check whether this is compensated inside the routine, but the per-point test
(\cref{sec:t4}) agrees with \ceex{} to $2.5\times10^{-5}$ of the tree. Some ``(-1)'' comments do
not match the code.

\section{Strategy}
\label{sec:strategy}

The difference is charge-odd ($C$-odd): it flips sign under $\pi^+\leftrightarrow\pi^-$ and
integrates to zero over the symmetric cuts. At $\mathcal O(\alpha^4)$ the $C$-odd part of the
cross section consists of the gauge-invariant classes $Q_e^5Q_\pi^3$ and $Q_e^3Q_\pi^5$ (the tree
interference is $Q_e^3Q_\pi^3$). Three tools were used to isolate it:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Cutoff independence.} The split into one-photon (Born + virtual + soft photons
  below the cutoff) and two-photon (hard) parts depends on the cutoff; the sum must not.
\item \textbf{Matched split.} For the same cutoff each part is uniquely defined, so the two codes
  must agree part by part. \phok{} $w=9.804\times10^{-6}$ ($w\sqrt s=10^{-5}$~GeV) matches
  \ceex{} $E_{\min}=10^{-5}$~GeV.
\item \textbf{Momentum swap.} At a phase-space point, the $C$-odd part of any piece $X$ is
  $X_{\rm odd}=\tfrac12[X(p_+,p_-)-X(p_-,p_+)]$. No charge parameter is needed.
\end{itemize}
Each histogram-level test used enough statistics to resolve $\Ath$ to $\sim10^{-3}$ while
checking that $\sigma$ and $M_{\pi\pi}$ are unchanged. The plan with all tests and hypotheses
is in \code{plan.txt}.

\section{The two errors in \phok}
\label{sec:bugs}

The structure of \code{ampLO} and the blocks B1--B5 are described in \cref{sec:phok-1ph} and
\cref{tab:phok-blocks}. Here $I$ and $F$ are the ISR and FSR tree amplitudes, $v_s$ the
electron-vertex correction at $s$ and $v_f$ the pion-vertex correction at $q^2$.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{H1: misplaced factor in the ISR$\times$FSR correction}{H1: misplaced factor in the ISR x FSR correction}}
\label{sec:h1}
\begin{lstlisting}
      if((nlo2.eq.1).and.(pion.eq.1))then
         ampLO = ampLO
     4     + dreal(
     5         dconjg(dme*(mb(1,1)-ma(1,1))*cvac_qq)*dme*(ddpl(1,1)-ddmi(1,1))*cvac_s   ! T1
     6       + dconjg(dme*(mb(2,2)-ma(2,2))*cvac_qq)*dme*(ddpl(2,2)-ddmi(2,2))*cvac_s   ! T2
     7       + dconjg((-ebppb*ma(2,1)+el_m2/ebppb*mb(2,1))*cvac_qq)
     8           *(-ebppb*ddmi(2,1)+el_m2/ebppb*ddpl(2,1))*cvac_s                       ! T3
     9       + dconjg((ebppb*mb(1,2)-el_m2/ebppb*ma(1,2))*cvac_qq)
     1           *(ebppb*ddpl(1,2)-el_m2/ebppb*ddmi(1,2))*cvac_s                        ! T4
     2           *(ver_f+ver_s) )       (*@\textbf{! multiplies T4 only}@*)
\end{lstlisting}
(Continuation lines re-indented for reading; the code is unchanged.)
The block is meant to be ${\rm Re}[T_1+T_2+T_3+T_4]\,(v_f+v_s)$, the amplitude-level corrections on
each side of $2{\rm Re}[I^*F]$. As written it is
${\rm Re}[T_1+T_2+T_3]+{\rm Re}[T_4](v_f+v_s)$, so ${\rm Re}[T_1+T_2+T_3]$ (about a third of the
tree interference) is added with coefficient 1. The muon version of the same block
(\code{phokhara\_10.0.f:10384--10394}) is correct:
\code{2*dreal(sum)*(ver\_s+ver\_f)/2}.

\textbf{Fix} (\code{variants/h1.patch}): \code{*cvac\_s *(ver\_f+ver\_s) )} $\to$
\code{*cvac\_s )*(ver\_f+ver\_s)}.

\subsection{H2: tree and analytic ISR soft function in the ISR one-loop block}
\label{sec:h2}
Block B5 contracts the FSR tree with the ISR current at one loop:
\begin{lstlisting}
      ampLO=ampLO+dreal( dconjg(... ddpl, ddmi ...)*( ... apl, ami ...) )    ! B5
\end{lstlisting}
The current (\code{hard\_soft\_part/ISRcurrent\_no\_mass\_terms.f}) has the coefficient
\begin{lstlisting}
      V1(1)=2.d0*log(4.d0*w**2)*(log(m2)+1.d0) -4.d0*log(qq2)+3.d0*log(m2) + ...   ! l.134
      V1(1)=(1.d0-2.d0*alpha/4.d0/pi*V1(1))/Sp                                   ! l.210
\end{lstlisting}
It therefore contains the tree (the ``1'') and the analytic ISR soft function, and B5 carries an
extra ${\rm Re}[F^*I]\,(1+S_{\rm ISR}(w))$ with
\[
S_{\rm ISR}(w)=\frac{\alpha}{\pi}\Big[-\ln(4w^2)\,(1+L)-\tfrac12L^2-L-\tfrac{\pi^2}{3}\Big],
\qquad L=\ln\frac{m_e^2}{s}.
\]
\begin{itemize}
\item The tree interference is already added by B3.
\item The analytic ISR soft function is replaced by the eikonal in
  \code{NLOpions}; it is subtracted from $v_s$, $v_f$ and the leptonic tensor, but not here.
\item At $w\simeq10^{-5}$ the two parts nearly cancel ($1+S_{\rm ISR}=0.044$), but
  $S_{\rm ISR}$ changes by $0.152$ per decade of $w$. This gives the T1 slope:
  $\tfrac12\cdot0.152\cdot A_{\rm LO}\approx0.019$ per decade, against $0.0156$ measured.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Fix} (\code{variants/h3.patch}): subtract ${\rm Re}[\sum T]\,(1+S_{\rm ISR})$ after B5, with
$S_{\rm ISR}$ computed as in the $v_s$ subtraction.

\section{Caveats and open points}
\label{sec:caveats}
\begin{itemize}
\item Only the configuration studied here was tested ($F_\pi=1$, no VP, KLOE-LA cuts). Both
  errors are in the pion full-NLO code whatever the form factor or VP setting, so
  they should also affect the Strong2020 form-factor results (there the NLO gap in $\Ath$ is
  $0.012$: \ceex{} $0.0804$, \phok{} $0.0924$). This has not been re-run.
\item After the fix, one of the three high-statistics samples ($w=10^{-4}$) lies $0.0007$ below the
  others; an independent sample at the same cutoff agrees with them and with \ceex{} part by part
  (\cref{sec:fix}), so it is treated as a fluctuation.
\item Muons take a different, correct branch and are not affected.
\item Totals and charge-even distributions are not affected by either error ($\sigma$ unchanged
  in every run; charge-even per-point agreement $10^{-5}$).
\item The $0.00027$ ($2\sigma$) excess of \ceex{} prod500k over \bby{} in $\Ath$ was a statistical
  fluctuation: \ceex{} more1M gives $\Ath=\CeexA$, equal to \bby{} ($0.23724(1)$) and the fixed
  \phok{} ($\FiveA$).
\item With $5\times10^9$ \phok{} events, small \bby--\phok{} shape differences become visible in a few
  charge-even observables ($\chi^2/{\rm ndf}$ 2--3 in $p_\pm$, $p^\perp_+$, $E_\gamma$; 10 in
  $|p^z_-|$, where \bby's binning already gave $\sim20$ before). \ceex{} agrees with \phok{} there at
  $\chi^2/{\rm ndf}\approx1$. They are unrelated to the pion-angle issue.
\item Tests T5 (charge-class split) and T6 (two-photon point-by-point) of the plan were not
  needed: the two-photon part agrees (T3) and H1+H2 explain the one-photon part (T4).
\end{itemize}

\section{Other findings}
\label{sec:other}
\begin{itemize}
\item The checked-out \code{PHOKARA\_10.0} has an empty stub \code{variables\_cuts.f} (no cuts, no
  histograms); the KLOE-I cut file comes from the Strong2020 scenario repository. The card format
  and the \code{FF\_pion} numbering changed with respect to the Strong2020 cards.
\item \phok{} aborts without writing histograms when a weight exceeds the scanned maximum
  (\code{phokhara\_10.0.f:1910,2135,2263}). Dropping such seeds, as the Strong2020 merge did,
  biases the result; for weighted events the abort is unnecessary.
\item \code{virt\_pions} overwrites its quad-precision momentum arguments ($\sim1$~GeV). This is
  harmless in production but breaks any second call in the same event.
\item In the 5 Aug reference folder, \code{Phokhara/NLOnovp\_*.csv} is the muon result
  ($0.9925$~nb), not the pion one.
\item \ceex: a few rank outputs per hundred diverge at 100k events (11/300); they are removed
  before merging. The \ceex-main README still documents \code{--ref\_alloc} and
  \code{--vfreeze}, which main no longer parses.
\item Cost: at equal precision on $\sigma$ (prod100k), \phok{} needed about 45 times less CPU
  than \ceex: $0.25$ against $28$ CPU-hours.
\end{itemize}
